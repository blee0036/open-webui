# Open Terminal attribution

The managed runtime extends [Open Terminal](https://github.com/open-webui/open-terminal), image version **0.17.7**,
copyright its contributors, under the MIT license included in the upstream image and repository.
The lifecycle controller, firewall and outbound proxy in this directory are local additions.
No enterprise Open WebUI Terminals orchestration implementation is included.

Open WebUI's own license, branding and notices remain applicable to the WebUI application.
